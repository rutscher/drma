WHAT IS GRVL DRMA?

🚴 The Race (Actually Important Info)

Distance: 120 Miles
Climbing: 11,000 Feet
Location: Plum City, Wisconsin
Cost: $0 (Actually FREE)
Aid Stations: Zero. Bring Your Own.
Vibe: Grassroots & Chill

A free grassroots gravel race through the extremely hilly Driftless region of Wisconsin. The route crosses several trout streams on lovely, winding roads through dense woodlands. The highlight? The gas station in Plum City with picnic tables and a pond filled with wildlife. Perfect for root beer with friends.

"I love climbing! My friends and I were tired of gravel races on flat, windy roads." - Ben, Race Organizer

💀 The Name (The Drama Explained)

GRVL DRMA exists because:

1️⃣ Gravel racing is full of drama - People arguing about "the spirit of gravel," whether attacking in the feed zone is okay, if pickle juice stashed on course is cheating, and everyone's favorite: aero bars discourse.

2️⃣ Bike racing is allergic to vowels - FNLD GRVL, UNBOUND, DK200, etc. We're just following tradition.

3️⃣ Inspired by Minnesota's free races - Following in the footsteps of the original Almanzo, Ragnarok 105, and Dickie Scramble.

🏔️ The Hilliest Possible Route

This is Laura Ingalls Wilder's stomping ground in the Driftless region of Wisconsin. The route is a mix of quiet paved and gravel roads with an emphasis on gravel and an unhealthy obsession with climbing.
