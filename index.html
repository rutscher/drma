FAQ

Why is it called GRVL DRMA?

Gravel racing is full of drama about "unwritten rules" - attacking in the feed zone, stashing pickle juice, using aero bars. The name is an homage to that drama, but we're actually drama-free. Also, bike racing is allergic to vowels.

Is it really free?

Yes! Registration is 100% free with no entry fees. We're inspired by Minnesota's grassroots gravel events like Almanzo and Ragnarok 105. As a bonus, we'll pay YOU a 0.0025 ETH (~$5 USD) participation reward just for registering! However, since all race bibs are NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain for permanent verification, you'll need to pay blockchain gas fees ($15-50 USD) for the minting transaction. So while registration itself is free, the net cost is approximately $10-45 depending on network congestion. But you get to keep your NFT bib forever!

Do I need a blockchain wallet to register?

Yes, a Web3 wallet is required for registration. All participants receive their race bib as an NFT on the Ethereum blockchain for permanent verification and results tracking. You'll need MetaMask, WalletConnect, or any Ethereum-compatible wallet. Don't worry - registration is completely free, and you'll even receive a 0.0025 ETH participation reward! (Note: Ethereum gas fees of $15-50 apply for the minting transaction, which are separate from registration and higher than the reward amount.)

Is GPS tracking required?

Yes. For safety and live race monitoring, all riders must enable GPS tracking via Garmin, Wahoo, or smartphone. Your position updates every 5 seconds to our real-time dashboard so friends, family, and SAG support can track you.

What about the AI gravel analysis?

Optional but recommended! Upload photos of your local gravel and our TensorFlow.js ML model will classify gravel types and provide optimal tire pressure recommendations. The model was trained on 10,000+ gravel images from Wisconsin's Driftless region.

How hard is 11,000 feet of climbing?

Very hard. The original route was 180 miles with 15,000' and it was too brutal. We scaled it back because we want to have fun out there. But make no mistake - this is an extremely hilly route. Power meter data helps us analyze your performance.

Can I use aero bars?

Use whatever you want. Aero bars, drop bars, mustache bars, no bars. We don't care. This is about having fun and riding bikes. Our AI-powered telemetry system doesn't judge your equipment choices.

What's the terrain like?

A mix of quiet paved and gravel roads with emphasis on gravel. Expect winding roads through dense woodlands, multiple trout stream crossings, and constant climbing. This is Laura Ingalls Wilder's stomping ground! All elevation data is stored in our database for historical analysis.
